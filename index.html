PhD Student · Research Software Engineer

Biomedical NLP · Information Retrieval · Semantic Web

I am a PhD student and a Research Software Engineer working at the intersection of biomedical natural language processing, text-to-structure transformation and information retrieval. My research focuses on developing AI pipelines, particularly for text-to-structured transformations such as nanopublications. I leverage large language models to generate structured representations of claims from text, enhancing provenance and explainability in the semantic web for the biomedical domain.

Current Focus

  • Biomedical named entity recognition and large-scale evaluation
  • Dense and hybrid information retrieval using domain-specific embeddings
  • Text-to-structure pipelines for scientific and biomedical literature
  • Benchmarking and deployment of NLP systems in production settings
